An Assessment of Innovation Capability of Artisanal Fabricators of Oil Palm Fruit Processing Machines in Nigeria

E.O. Ojo, G.O. Binuyo, B.S. Ogunsina, O.K. Owolarafe

Abstract

This research assesses the innovation capability of artisanal fabricators involved in the design and manufacture of oil palm fruit processing machines across Nigeria. The study examines how local fabricators innovate within resource-constrained environments, contributing to the agricultural technology landscape in developing economies.

The findings provide insights into the intersection of indigenous innovation, technology adoption, and manufacturing capability in the Nigerian agricultural processing sector, with implications for policy, technology transfer, and capacity building.

Key Research Areas

Significance

This paper contributes to the understanding of grassroots innovation in developing economies, particularly in how artisanal fabricators adapt and innovate processing technologies to meet local agricultural needs. The research has practical implications for technology policy, SME development, and agricultural productivity improvement in Nigeria and West Africa.
