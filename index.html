About

Where technology meets commerce

Emmanuel is a strategic business leader with over 15 years of experience in technology, sales and business development across the IT education sector. He is Acting Chief Sales & Marketing Officer at New Horizons, leading growth across university partnerships and corporate training.

He holds a PhD in Technology Management and an MBA in Strategic Management, graduated with First Class honours in Management Information Systems from Covenant University, and earned his MSc with Distinction as a PTDF Scholar. For a decade he has also taught the next generation of IT professionals at Covenant and Babcock Universities, among others.

He is a Mentor on the Tony Elumelu Foundation 2025 cohort, and a registered member of the Computer Professionals Registration Council of Nigeria (CPN) and the Nigeria Computer Society (NCS).
