Design and Development of an E-Petition Mobile Application

Abstract

This paper presents the design and development of an e-petition mobile application aimed at enhancing citizen participation in governance and civic engagement. The research explores the technical architecture, user experience design, and implementation of a mobile platform that enables citizens to create, sign, and track petitions digitally.

The application addresses the growing need for accessible digital tools that empower citizens to engage with governance processes, particularly in developing nations where traditional petition mechanisms may be limited or inaccessible.

Key Research Areas

  • Mobile Application Development
  • E-Governance
  • Civic Technology
  • User Experience Design
  • Citizen Engagement
  • Digital Democracy

Significance

This research contributes to the growing body of work on civic technology by demonstrating how mobile applications can bridge the gap between citizens and governance processes. The e-petition platform provides a scalable, accessible solution for digital civic participation, with potential applications across emerging democracies.

The paper was presented at the 2021 AISPI International Biennial Conference and published in the conference proceedings (pages 105–118), reflecting its relevance to the intersection of information systems and public interest.
